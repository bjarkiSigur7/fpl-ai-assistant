### Confirmed 2026-27 rule changes and features

From [premierleague.com official changes article](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679873/all-you-need-to-know-about-changes-to-fpl-for-202627) and [FFS's summary](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/07/20/fpl-2026-27-5-rule-changes-new-features-announced):

1. **BPS tweaks** (affects bonus modeling, not base points): the −1 BPS per "being tackled" deduction is **removed**; CBI (clearances/blocks/interceptions) now earn 1 BPS per **3** actions (was per 2); GK saves rebalanced — outside-box saves no longer earn extra BPS, standard saves 2 BPS, saving a "big chance" +1 BPS. Stated intent: reduce BPS/DefCon overlap, boost GKs, attacking full-backs, creative players.
2. **Defensive Contribution (DefCon) points continue unchanged** from 2025-26 ([premierleague.com DefCon article](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4361991/whats-happening-with-defensive-contribution-points-in-202627-fantasy)).
3. **Chips unchanged: two full sets** (Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost, Triple Captain), one per half-season; first set expires at the **GW19 deadline, 13:30 GMT Saturday 2 January 2027**; no carry-over. (2025-26 bootstrap confirms same structure: each chip with start/stop events 1-19 and 20-38.)
4. **Transfers: 1 FT/GW, bankable to a maximum of 5** (unchanged; API `max_extra_free_transfers = 4`). −4 per extra transfer. No AFCON bonus transfers this season.
5. **Live projected bonus** shown after 20 minutes of each fixture; **live mini-league and overall rank updates** during gameweeks.
6. **Later gameweek "lockdown":** scores now finalize at **09:00 UK time the day after the GW's final match** (was ~1h after full-time) to let Opta corrections land. Data pipelines must not treat overnight point totals as final.
7. **New site features:** official **Price Change Predictor** (price changes at 00:00 UK), squad-view overlays (FDR, ownership, form), percentile career ranks, AI gameweek recaps.
